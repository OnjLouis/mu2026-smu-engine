Live Playback

Boots the synth in an AudioWorklet and plays it from WebMIDI inputs (up to 4 ports, A–D) in realtime. You need ROMs extracted from your own MU2000 (see Preparing the ROMs); they are stored locally in your browser and never uploaded anywhere. Works best in the Firefox browser.
